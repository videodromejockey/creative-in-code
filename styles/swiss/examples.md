# Swiss style: examples

These treatments worked in earlier projects. They are ideas, not a required toolkit. A newer tool or a better method is always allowed. The earlier projects used Python with skia-python (drawing), NumPy (image work) and fontTools (fonts), plus ffmpeg for video.

Add each new project to the table: its name, its medium, its script and its outputs. Paths are relative to the work folder.

| Project | Medium | Script | Outputs |
|---|---|---|---|

## Treatments

- **Circle packing** and a **triangle mosaic**.
- **Contours and ridge lines**, with hidden lines removed.
- **Ridge lines with the subject in the accent colour.**
- **Chronophotography:** repeated positions of the subject in one frame.
- **Woodcut hatching.**
- **Vector trace and motion paths.**
- **Measured plate:** the subject with dimensions, angles and a key.
- **Dot screen**, and a **45° dot screen**.
- **Riso, bitmap, scan lines, strokes, rays.**
- **Halftone, riso, vesica, contour, letters.**
- **Two-ink riso with grain, isolines, threshold cut-out with rings, scan lines.**
- **Silkscreen, engraving, silhouette, squares, weave.**
- **Depth terraces from user scribbles:** random-walker zones, colour by depth.
- **Motion with a lingering area that ends on the still:** isoline bloom and flow, ring pulse and drift, scan beam and tear, a red-only VHS or DVD glitch.
- **A rigged 3D character as scan lines:** 12 fps on twos, VHS tears, footprints, pulse rings, a texture per shot (horizontal, vertical, Bayer cells, 45° hatching, rings), onion-skin ghosts, a beam wipe, match cuts on footfalls.
